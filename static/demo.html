Complaint Registration

Citizens report infrastructure defects in seconds

📸

Upload a Defect Photo

The citizen snaps a photo of the pothole, broken pipe, dangling wire, or garbage dump. The image is immediately sent to the OpenCV AI engine for defect severity analysis.

🧠

AI Analyzes Severity in Real-Time

Computer Vision detects crack edges, crater contours, and dark voids. The NLP engine scans the description for safety keywords like "hospital", "school", "live wire".

⚡

Instant Triage Result

Within seconds, the citizen sees the computed Priority Score (e.g., 96/100 CRITICAL), the SLA deadline (< 6 hours), and the department automatically assigned (Roads & PWD).

5

Easy Identification

Locate defects even without a street address

Many potholes exist in areas with no clear street address — unmarked lanes, internal colony roads, service roads under flyovers. CivicPulse solves this:

📍

GPS Pin + Satellite Overlay

The red pin is shown on high-res satellite imagery. Authorities can see exactly which lane, gate, or divider the defect is near.

🏫

Nearby Landmark Anchoring

Buildings, schools, hospitals, and metro stations visible on the satellite map act as reference landmarks — even without a formal address.

🔴

65-Meter De-Duplication Buffer

Dashed circles around each complaint show the spatial zone. Multiple reports in the same area automatically merge — confirming the location and boosting priority.

📝

Access Instructions

"Left lane, 10m before Victoria emergency ramp entrance" — citizens add gate notes that help field crews navigate the last few meters.

Future Scope & Technological Roadmap

Scaling capabilities, emerging technology integrations, and long-term civic impact

🛠️

1. Solution Improvements & Features

  • Edge-AI Offline App: On-device YOLOv8 / ONNX inference for instant defect detection without cellular network.
  • Multilingual Voice Intake: Speech-to-text in regional Indian languages (Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu) with automatic hazard extraction.
  • WhatsApp / Telegram Civic Bot: Report defects via photo and WhatsApp live-location without requiring app installation.
  • Dynamic Contractor Penalty & Reward: Automated liquidated damages linked to AI Before-After repair contour audit.
🌐

2. Scaling & Multi-Sector Expansion

  • Pan-India Municipalities: Multi-tenant rollout to BMC Mumbai, GHMC Hyderabad, GCC Chennai, and NDMC Delhi.
  • National & State Highways (NHAI): Real-time corridor defect monitoring across thousands of highway kilometers.
  • Transit & Rail Infrastructure: Inspection of Metro viaducts, railway track fissures, and catenary power lines.
  • Smart Campuses & SEZs: Private industrial estates, tech corridors (ITPL/Electronic City), and airport facilities.
🤖

3. Advanced Tech (IoT, Drones & Blockchain)

  • Autonomous Bus & Fleet AI Dashcams: BMTC buses and garbage tippers continuously scan 100% of roads passively.
  • IoT Acoustic Leak & Vibration Sensors: Pipeline flow sensors and roadbed accelerometers detect bursts before cavities form.
  • Blockchain Proof-of-Work: Immutable ledger recording ticket timestamps, contractor dispatch, and before/after verification hashes.
  • Generative AI Dispatch Copilot: LLM agents automatically generate contractor work orders, asphalt volume estimates, and BOMs.
📈

4. Real-World Deployment & Impact

  • SLA Slashed from 21 Days to < 24 Hours: Automated triage routes directly to zonal field crews with 0 administrative lag.
  • Zero Pothole Fatalities: Eliminates two-wheeler skids and ambulance delays around sensitive transit corridors.
  • Massive Fiscal Savings: Deduplication prevents duplicate payments; prevents ghost billings via visual AI audits.
  • Data-Driven Urban Planning: Heatmap intelligence identifies recurring structural road failure zones for long-term re-laying.
